Level design

The game consists of 4 main levels:

The maze room: Involves collaborating with your teammate to clear a maze. One stays within the maze while the teammate guides the teammate from a dock in the sky, all while solving puzzles to clear pathways.

The rotarium: The players are trapped in a room. Each time they press a button, the gravity switches and both players risk falling. They must collaborate to click the buttons in the right sequence and solve the room.

The separation room: Divided by a glass wall, both players pass each other objects that help them solve puzzles inside each others’ rooms.

The gun room: The final test, which evaluates your loyalty to your teammate.
