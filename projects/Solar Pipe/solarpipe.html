A solar light pipe functions by capturing and funneling light from the exterior to an interior space.

In this project, the aim was to design a bespoke form and fitting that complements the atrium space at the Ark Foundation Museum, Vadodara.

Research and Conception

Targeted space in the museum

Initially the design was intended to function as a sundial, mapping the movement of the shadows to the amount of light that would come in through each light pipe. In the end, this effect was much more subdued. The following images briefly outline the design process.
